# Sample Sports Knowledge

## NBA Teams 2026

The NBA continues to feature 30 teams across two conferences. Key contenders this season include:

- **Los Angeles Lakers**: Led by veteran stars, maintaining playoff position
- **Boston Celtics**: Strong defensive team with championship aspirations
- **Golden State Warriors**: Dynasty team still competitive with experienced core
- **Denver Nuggets**: Rising team with MVP-caliber center Nikola Jokic

## NBA Statistics Glossary

**PPG** - Points Per Game: Average points scored by a player per game
**RPG** - Rebounds Per Game: Average rebounds collected per game
**APG** - Assists Per Game: Average assists made per game
**FG%** - Field Goal Percentage: (Made shots / Attempted shots) × 100
**3P%** - Three-Point Percentage: Success rate from beyond the arc
**PER** - Player Efficiency Rating: Comprehensive statistic measuring per-minute performance

## NFL Conference Structure

The NFL consists of 32 teams divided into two conferences:

**AFC (American Football Conference)**:
- East: Patriots, Bills, Dolphins, Jets
- North: Ravens, Steelers, Browns, Bengals
- South: Titans, Colts, Jaguars, Texans
- West: Chiefs, Chargers, Raiders, Broncos

**NFC (National Football Conference)**:
- East: Cowboys, Eagles, Commanders, Giants
- North: Packers, Vikings, Bears, Lions
- South: Saints, Buccaneers, Falcons, Panthers
- West: 49ers, Seahawks, Rams, Cardinals

## Soccer Premier League

Top English football league featuring 20 teams. Notable clubs:

- **Manchester United**: Historic club with global fanbase
- **Liverpool**: Recent champions with passionate supporters
- **Manchester City**: Dominant force with tactical excellence
- **Chelsea**: London-based club with European success
- **Arsenal**: Traditional powerhouse in North London

## Key Sports Terminology

**Playoff**: Post-season tournament to determine champion
**Regular Season**: Main portion of season where teams compete for playoff spots
**Division**: Group of teams in same conference/region
**Wild Card**: Playoff spot for non-division winners with best records
**Home Advantage**: Benefit of playing at team's own venue
